Field training breaks down in three predictable places

These are the gaps Capacity Connect was built to close, not generic training-portal features.

Records live outside the system

Registers and spreadsheets sit disconnected from what officers actually do in the field, so nobody has one accurate picture of who's trained on what.

Gaps surface too late

A skill gap usually shows up during an audit or an incident — long after there was time to close it through targeted training.

Connectivity stalls learning

Field posts lose signal for days at a stretch, and training simply stops until the network comes back.

Built around how the work actually happens

Field-first offline mode

Training that doesn't stop when the network does

Modules, quizzes, notes and progress all work offline. The moment a device reconnects, everything syncs automatically — no lost progress, no manual re-upload.

    How training moves through the portal

    1. Station head

      Nominates officers

      Picks officers who need a skill, with a reason.

    2. Training Division

      Approves the batch

      Assigns a trainer and a start date.

    3. Trainee

      Learns, online or off

      Lessons, quizzes and a practical at the station.

    4. Trainer / Mentor

      Reviews and scores

      Answers questions and scores practical work.

    5. Record

      Certificate and APAR entry

      A verifiable record of real competency.

    Shaped by field officers

    We spoke with officers working at IMD stations. Their feedback changed the design in five concrete ways.

    1. Nobody enrols in training unchecked. Station heads nominate, officers can request a course, and the Training Division approves every batch.
    2. Modules follow real field duties: AWS and surface instruments, Radar, Satellite data analysis, and Aviation.
    3. Administrators filter every view by Regional Met Centre, Met Centre and field observatory.
    4. Pages stay light enough for low-bandwidth coastal and remote stations.
    5. Training is a conversation, so mentor discussion sits right beside each lesson.
